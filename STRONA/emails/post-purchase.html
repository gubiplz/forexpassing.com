From application to first trade
No account is touched before step three is done.
1 We review your application. Part of that is checking your prop firm: we only work with firms whose rules allow a third party to manage the account. If yours does not, we say so and name firms that do.
2 A short conversation. Email or Telegram, whichever you prefer. We go through the firm, the account size, the risk rules you are held to and what you already have.
3 A written management agreement. Profit split, risk limits, reporting and what happens if we break the firm's rules — on paper, signed, before anyone logs in.
4 We trade, you get reports. A typical evaluation runs about 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the firm's minimum trading days, its drawdown rules and market conditions. Trading carries risk and no outcome is guaranteed.
