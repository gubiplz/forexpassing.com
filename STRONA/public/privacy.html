Privacy Policy

In force from 2 August 2026. This document has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It describes what we actually collect and what we do with it.

1. Who controls your data

The data controller is Forex Passing. Full controller details (registered office, company number, contact email) are provided on written request.

2. Data protection officer

We have not appointed a data protection officer; the scale and nature of our processing does not require one. Data protection questions go to the contact address published on this site.

3. What we collect

The information you type into the application questionnaire, plus basic technical details of the visit:

  • from the questionnaire: your name, email address and, optionally, phone number and country;
  • from the questionnaire: your answers to the qualification questions: your experience with prop firm evaluations, when you would want to start, whether you are able to fund an evaluation, and how you prefer to be contacted;
  • from the questionnaire: the bracket your monthly income falls into. We ask for a range, never an exact figure, and only to judge whether the service makes sense for you;
  • your IP address and browser User-Agent string, stored alongside the submission;
  • analytics collected by Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, under those providers' own terms.

We do not collect anything from social media accounts and we never ask for card details. No payment is taken through this site.

4. Why we use it

Questionnaire data is used to contact you about your enquiry, to check the rules of the prop firm you named, and to prepare a management agreement. Analytics data is used to measure how our advertising performs.

5. Legal basis

For questionnaire data: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For analytics and advertising: your consent, or our legitimate interest in measuring our own marketing (Art. 6(1)(a) and (f) GDPR).

6. Who it reaches

Submissions go to the Forex Passing team. We use infrastructure and analytics providers (hosting, email, Google Analytics, Meta) who process the data only on our instructions. We do not sell your data and do not release it for third-party marketing.

Some of those providers are based outside the European Economic Area; those transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for 24 months after the last contact. If an agreement is signed, data is kept for its duration plus the period required by tax law.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy in a portable format. Email the address published on this site and we answer within 30 days.

You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority at any time, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). You do not have to complain to us first, though it is usually faster.

9. Cookies

The site uses cookies that are necessary for it to work, and analytics and advertising cookies set by us and by Google and Meta. Non-essential cookies are used on the basis of consent, which you can withdraw at any time by clearing cookies for this site and blocking them in your browser.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we update the date at the top and, where the change affects people who have already applied, we tell them directly.
