What is a prop firm evaluation?

A proprietary trading firm evaluation (often called a challenge) is a paid assessment in which a trader must hit a profit target on a demo account without breaking the firm's risk rules, such as daily and overall drawdown limits. Traders who pass are given access to a funded account and keep a share of the profits they make on it.

How do drawdown limits and profit targets work?

A profit target is the percentage gain you must reach to pass, for example 8–10%. A daily drawdown limit caps how much the account may lose in a single day, and an overall drawdown limit caps the total loss from the starting balance — cross either one and the evaluation is failed, regardless of earlier profits. Understanding these limits, and sizing positions so a normal losing streak cannot breach them, is the core skill an evaluation tests.

Is forex trading risky?

Yes. Forex is traded with leverage, which magnifies both gains and losses, and a large share of retail traders lose money. You should never trade with funds you cannot afford to lose, and you remain responsible for your own trading decisions at all times.
