What we will ask you

What to bring to the call

Rough answers are fine — nothing has to be decided before we speak. If you do not have an account yet, say so; picking the firm is part of the call.

  • Which prop firm. The one you already trade with, or the one you are considering. We check whether its rules allow a third party to trade the account.
  • Account size and stage. Evaluation or already funded, the account size, and the daily loss and drawdown limits that come with it.
  • Your experience so far. How long you have traded, which challenges you have taken, and what went wrong on the ones that failed.
  • Your timezone and Telegram handle, so we can book a slot that works and keep reporting in one place.
