The service is provided by Forex Passing. Full registration details (legal entity, address, company number) are stated in the management agreement and given on request.

2. What the service is

We trade an evaluation or funded account that belongs to you, opened at a proprietary trading firm, within that firm's own risk rules.

You buy the evaluation yourself and remain the account owner. We receive trading access only, under a written management agreement — never ownership of the account or of the funds in it.

We only work with firms whose rules permit a third party to trade the account. We check this before starting and decline the engagement if the firm forbids it.

3. What it costs

There is no upfront fee and no subscription. Our fee is 30% of a payout released to you by the prop firm; you keep 70%.

The fee becomes due only after the payout has actually reached you. No payout means no fee.

Submitting the questionnaire on this site is an enquiry. It creates no contract and no financial obligation for either side. A contract exists only once both parties have signed a separate management agreement. Until then we have no access to your account.

The guarantee runs for 120 days from the date the management agreement is signed. An evaluation that ends in a loss while the firm's rules were respected is not a breach — market risk is inherent to the service.

6. Your obligations

You agree to give accurate information, not to interfere with positions we have opened, and to tell us promptly if your prop firm changes its rules or contacts you about the account.

7. Ending the engagement

Either side may terminate on the notice period stated in the management agreement. You can revoke our access at any moment by changing your trading platform password.
